## Product purpose

SumUSD is an over-collateralized, aggregated USD stablecoin. Users deposit whitelisted,
GENIUS-Act-compliant stablecoin "flavors" and mint a single fungible dollar token (SumUSD); they
burn it to redeem any flavor the pool holds. This site (sumusd.com) is the mint/redeem dapp plus a
short explainer of how the protocol works.

## Users

On-chain stablecoin holders and DeFi users, comfortable with wallets, approvals, slippage, and
redemption mechanics. They arrive to mint or redeem, and to check system health (collateralization,
redemption rate). Task-first, not browsing.

## Brand & tone

Sober, exact, trustworthy. SumUSD is financial infrastructure, not a hype coin. Plain language,
precise numbers, no marketing adjectives. The mark is a green circle with a white Sigma (Σ), for
"sum". Green signals aggregation and health, used sparingly as the single accent over a near-white
surface.

## Anti-references

- Neon-on-black "crypto" aesthetics: glow, gradients, animated gradients.
- Hype copy, rocket emoji, APY-bait, countdowns.
- Hero-metric dashboards with giant gradient numbers.
- Anything that reads as a memecoin or a yield farm.

## Strategic principles

- Trust through clarity: show backing, rate, and what you receive before any action.
- Honest states: paused minting, under-collateralized pro-rata redemption, and depegs are surfaced
  plainly, never hidden.
- The interface disappears into the task: one column, one decision at a time.
